# Tidemark Station 2.0 — Voxel Coast

A standalone interactive coastal story. This is a new game project, not a portfolio or project catalogue. The existing seoshiro portfolio and archived Signal implementation are out of scope and must remain untouched.

The visitor arrives after a storm and restores a lighthouse before the fictional high tide. Three independently solvable mechanisms form the main game loop: match copper rotors to their pale guides, focus a lens within 60-68, then repeat the visible code 1-3-2. An optional salvage route opens three wooden crates and returns a copper coil, glass prism and keeper's chart to an inventory. The beacon and journal remain available without collecting salvage. Completing the optional kit restores the beacon's signal crown. All readings and narrative details are fictional, including the 12-minute label; there is no countdown.

## Visual system

Night water #0c192d, limestone #c5c4a0, grass #617e42, copper #c48044, glass #4cabb0 and warm light #ffe1a0. Onest UI and Manrope display are hosted locally. This revision replaces the flat faceted language with original textured cubic geometry: stepped cliffs, a block-built lighthouse with layered copper roof, glass gallery, keeper's hut, pines, timber pier and boat. A genuine perspective camera projects 3D vertices, culls backfaces and orders depth. Seeded 16×16 pixel textures are authored in code. Top faces are brighter; side faces and cast shadows share upper-left lighting. No Minecraft assets, sounds, fonts or branding are used.

## Scenes and movement

The shore establishes purpose, a strong tower silhouette and an obvious descent. One courier lantern, rendered once in a shared overlay, travels from the pier to a boat between three islands, then to the keeper's book and the final optical assembly. Its projected screen anchors blend continuously and settle with a short lag. Desktop workshop uses one 280svh reversible pinned scene with normalized progress 0-1. Modules descend onto islands, compress, emit up to 30 dust cubes and settle at contact. On phones the overview and three larger closeups accompany the same native mechanism controls without pinning. The quiet journal opens physically around its spine; page changes alter its chart markings and native readable text. The Fresnel slices and copper shutters assemble/open reversibly with scroll. Saved work is separate from these poses.

Native wheel, touch, keyboard and anchors retain control. A shared RAF samples actual displacement/time, smooths velocity with 85ms attack and 380ms release and steps local damped physical reactions. Logical pickup/activation timing uses actual visible elapsed time; physics uses bounded integration steps. Rendering targets 30fps and caps DPR at 1.25. Static meshes, projected snapshots and textures are reused; moving boats/modules remain separate. Canvas clocks and drawing stop out of view. All simulation pauses on document hiding. Reduced motion removes pinning, parallax, physical impacts, page turns and item flight while preserving the game, loot and stable scene poses.

## Sound and state

One opt-in AudioContext has music, ambience and SFX buses into master. The score is a quiet original D/A/D pad. Filtered procedural air follows actual smoothed scroll velocity; a low hum follows charging. Stone contact uses low-pass noise, timber lids use resonant band-pass noise, paper uses high-pass noise, copper uses a short descending triangle, glass rings at 1174.66 Hz, and recovered items have three clean pitched variants. These are synthesis descriptions, not a claim of listening. SFX polyphony is capped at four, with cooldowns and a priority climax reservation. Music ducks before the accent and recovers slowly. The visual contact follows the same short reservation interval. Sound/volume and motion remain independent; re-enable reuses the same context/sources. Unsupported audio leaves the game playable.

World state supplies scene, progress/direction/velocity, projected pointer/focus proximity, mechanisms, opening, journal/page, chest poses, pending collection, item flight, per-scene clocks, charge and settings. A crate grants its item once at opening; the item flies to its slot, which brightens at arrival. Reload during flight restores the earned item without replaying or re-granting it. The mobile inventory becomes compact for reading but stays expanded during transfers. Reset cancels pending collection, flight and climax. The existing `tidemark-save-v1` key migrates old achievements and validates/deduplicates new item IDs. Storage denial falls back to memory; malformed data cannot restore an unearned lit beacon.
